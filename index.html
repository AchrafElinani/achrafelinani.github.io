About Me

I'm an undergraduate studying Electrical and Computer Engineering at The Ohio State University, with minors in Economics and English. My work and academic interests are centered on technology: how it is designed, manufactured, commercialized, and shaped by public policy.

Through work in semiconductor manufacturing and battery research, I've gained experience with the infrastructure and processes behind emerging technologies. I also enjoy studying the economic forces that influence where industries grow and how innovation reaches the market.

Outside engineering, I pursue research and writing about industrial policy, international development, and the relationship between technology and society.
